# Skowak case studies

Twenty years of turning operationally dense domains into systems people use:
complex rules, regulated constraints, measured outcomes.

## Fidelity Life — insurance underwriting

**Taking insurance applications from four days to thirty minutes.** Underwriting,
case management, and product configuration across a multi-year transformation of
a life insurance carrier's application process.
Full case study: https://skowak.com/work/fidelity-life/

## Bitscopic — federal healthcare data

**Cutting clinical data review effort by up to 80%.** A suite of federal
healthcare data products (2013–2014) built for Bitscopic, supporting clinical
and operational review inside a government health system under the constraints
that apply when the data is patient data and the users are clinicians. Skowak
designed the health-data analysis, reporting and decision-support interfaces —
including Praedico, surveillance and operational review across sites, built for
clinicians rather than analysts. Issue detection and data-exploration effort
reduced by 50–80%.
Full case study: https://skowak.com/work/federal-health/

## SunPower — commercial solar design

**Compressing commercial solar design from days to 25 minutes.** UX architecture
and product design for SunPower's digital platform, covering solar layout,
production impact, bills of materials, and proposal generation.
Full case study: https://skowak.com/work/sunpower/

## Craftal — multi-agent product planning

**A research-based multi-agent product-planning system.** A custom DSL and RAG
pipeline behind a deterministic verification core, turning product context into
structured, verifiable planning workflows with human review built in. Built and
operated by Skowak.
Full case study: https://skowak.com/work/craftal/
