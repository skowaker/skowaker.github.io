Skowak

What is Skowak?

Skowak is an AI transformation boutique that builds production AI systems for operationally complex, regulated businesses. It is built on twenty years of designing and shipping software for domains that resist it: insurance underwriting, federal healthcare data, solar engineering, and developer infrastructure.

Who does Skowak work with?

Organizations with an AI project stalled between prototype and production, whose operation is genuinely complex — regulated, multi-stakeholder, or built on decades of accumulated process — and who need one person who can design the system and build it rather than one or the other. Skowak works with startups from garage to Series A and with enterprises of thousands of employees.

Who is Skowak not for?

Teams that need a large delivery team or 24/7 coverage, teams that want a strategy deck with no implementation, and buyers shopping primarily on hourly rate. For a large multi-year program, hire a firm.

What makes Skowak different?

The person who runs discovery is the person who builds the system. No translation loss, no staffing surprise, no junior team learning the client's domain on the client's budget. Engagements are structured around handoff rather than dependency — the goal is to make Skowak unnecessary as quickly as possible.

What domains has Skowak worked in?

Insurance (Fidelity Life), federal healthcare data (Bitscopic / VA), renewable energy (SunPower), developer tooling and DevOps automation (StackStorm), cloud infrastructure (Engine Yard), education (Colearn), and consulting delivery (ThoughtWorks, Microsoft).

What AI systems has Skowak built for itself?

Craftal, a research-based multi-agent product-planning system with a custom DSL and RAG pipeline behind a deterministic verification core. Finetunio, model development, evaluation and control as a single connected flow. CareDash, a multi-agent healthcare workflow and decision-support system with human-in-the-loop review. Contextus, a model-agnostic AI workspace for portable context and user-owned memory. These are live systems, not demos, and much of the applied-AI practice used in client work was developed in them first.
