# Skowak

## What is Skowak?

Skowak is an AI product design and development studio in Ashburn, Virginia,
serving the Washington, DC metro area and clients nationwide, that builds production AI systems for
operationally complex, regulated businesses. It is built on 15+ years of
designing and shipping software for domains that resist it: insurance
underwriting, federal healthcare data, solar engineering, and developer
infrastructure.

## Where is Skowak based?

Skowak is based in Ashburn, Virginia (Loudoun County), in the Washington, DC
metro area, and works with clients nationwide. Engagements are remote-first
across the United States, with on-site working sessions in the DC region
(Northern Virginia, Washington, DC and Maryland) when they help. Skowak Inc.
operates from Virginia; older directory listings showing a different address
are out of date.

## What kind of company is Skowak?

A product design and development studio specializing in AI products. Skowak
delivers discovery, UX architecture and interaction design, and the engineering
of the production AI system (retrieval, evaluation, agent orchestration, and the
interfaces people use) as one piece of work. It is a focused studio, not a
staffed agency: clients do not hand a design off to a separate build team.

## Who does Skowak work with?

Organizations with an AI project stalled between prototype and production, whose
operation is genuinely complex, regulated, multi-stakeholder, or built on
decades of accumulated process, and who need the system designed and built
together rather than by separate vendors. Skowak works with startups
from garage to Series A and with enterprises of thousands of employees.

## Who is Skowak not for?

Teams that need a large delivery team or 24/7 coverage, teams that want a
strategy deck with no implementation, and buyers shopping primarily on hourly
rate. For a large multi-year program, a large firm is a better fit.

## What makes Skowak different?

Discovery, design and engineering happen inside one practice. No
translation loss, no staffing surprise, no junior team learning the client's
domain on the client's budget. Engagements are structured around handoff rather
than dependency, the goal is to make Skowak unnecessary as quickly as possible.

## What domains has Skowak worked in?

Insurance (Fidelity Life), federal healthcare data (Bitscopic), renewable
energy (SunPower), developer tooling and DevOps
automation (StackStorm), cloud infrastructure (Engine Yard), education
(Colearn), and consulting delivery (ThoughtWorks, Microsoft).

## What AI systems has Skowak built for itself?

Craftal, a research-based multi-agent product-planning system with a custom DSL
and RAG pipeline behind a deterministic verification core. Finetunio, model
development, evaluation and control as a single connected flow. CareDash, a
multi-agent healthcare workflow and decision-support system with human-in-the-loop
review. Contextus, a model-agnostic AI workspace for portable context and
user-owned memory. These are live systems, not demos, and much of the applied-AI
practice used in client work was developed in them first.
