Skowak engagements

Three tiers, one principle: nothing counts until it is running in the client's business and their team can maintain it. A client can enter at any tier.

Tier one — AI Opportunity Sprint

2–3 weeks · fixed fee · starts at $12,000

Most AI projects fail at scoping, not at engineering. The Sprint front-loads that risk: Skowak goes through the operation, finds where AI can actually create value, and pressure-tests it against the client's data, constraints and people before the first line of code is written.

Deliverables: a prioritized opportunity heatmap (what to build, in what order, and why); a feasibility assessment per candidate with the projects that should not be built called out and explained; an evaluation framework blueprint; a stakeholder alignment summary; and honest scope, sequence and cost estimates.

The deliverable belongs to the client whether or not the engagement continues. If the Sprint concludes a project should not be built, that is the cheapest useful answer available.

Tier two — Agentic Workflow Redesign

4–6 weeks · fixed scope · starts at $35,000

For when an AI pilot works in a demo and someone has to figure out how it fits the actual workflow: who reviews what the agent produces, what happens when it is wrong, how anyone knows it is working, and who is accountable. That is a design problem, not an engineering problem.

Deliverables: current-state and future-state workflow maps distinguishing where human judgment is genuinely required from where it is merely habitual; agentic workflow blueprints with agent placement, human-in-the-loop checkpoints and escalation paths; an evaluation framework for every AI-assisted decision point; a RACI matrix and traceability diagrams for governance and audit; and a pilot action plan that bridges to implementation.

Tier three — Embedded Build & Prove Retainer

Monthly · minimum 3 months · starts at $35,000/month

Skowak works inside the client's team. The system gets designed, built, instrumented and proved, and Skowak stays until it is in production, the client's people can maintain it, and there is a plan for what comes next. This is not advisory — Skowak writes code, reviews the team's code, and owns delivery.

Deliverables: a production AI system built end to end; evaluation harnesses; a performance dashboard tracking accuracy, reliability, adoption and business impact; interface and workflow design; monthly retrospectives; and a scale-up roadmap with ownership transfer in the final month.

Month-to-month after the initial three months. If the evaluation dashboard shows the work is not producing results, the client stops.

What every engagement produces

Working software in the client's repository under their control; evaluation suites and ground-truth datasets they own; architecture and decision documentation; interface designs and workflow models; recorded walkthroughs for the team who inherits it; and a written record of what was ruled out and why.
