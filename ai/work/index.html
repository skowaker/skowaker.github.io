Federal healthcare data — clinical and operational review

Cutting clinical data review effort by up to 80%. A suite of federal healthcare data products (2013–2014) supporting clinical and operational review inside a government health system, built under the constraints that apply when the data is patient data and the users are clinicians. Skowak designed the health-data analysis, reporting and decision-support interfaces — including Praedico, surveillance and operational review across sites, built for clinicians rather than analysts. Issue detection and data-exploration effort reduced by 50–80%. The client is not named on this case study. Full case study: https://skowak.com/work/federal-health/

SunPower — commercial solar design

Compressing commercial solar design from days to 25 minutes. UX architecture and product design for SunPower's digital platform, covering solar layout, production impact, bills of materials, and proposal generation. Full case study: https://skowak.com/work/sunpower/

Craftal — multi-agent product planning

A research-based multi-agent product-planning system. A custom DSL and RAG pipeline behind a deterministic verification core, turning product context into structured, verifiable planning workflows with human review built in. Built and operated by Skowak. Full case study: https://skowak.com/work/craftal/
