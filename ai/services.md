# Skowak engagements

Two categories. AI Design & Development is three tiers on one principle:
nothing counts until it is running in the client's business and their team can
maintain it. A client can enter at any tier. AI Fluency is a separate,
audience-specific enablement track — see below.

## Tier one, AI Opportunity Sprint

**2–3 weeks · fixed fee**

Most AI projects fail at scoping, not at engineering. The Sprint front-loads
that risk: Skowak goes through the operation, finds where AI can actually create
value, and pressure-tests it against the client's data, constraints and people
before the first line of code is written.

Deliverables: a prioritized opportunity heatmap (what to build, in what order,
and why); a feasibility assessment per candidate with the projects that should
*not* be built called out and explained; an evaluation framework blueprint;
a stakeholder alignment summary; and honest scope, sequence and cost estimates.

The deliverable belongs to the client whether or not the engagement continues.
If the Sprint concludes a project should not be built, that is the cheapest
useful answer available.

## Tier two, Agentic Workflow Redesign

**4–6 weeks · fixed scope**

For when an AI pilot works in a demo and someone has to figure out how it fits
the actual workflow: who reviews what the agent produces, what happens when it
is wrong, how anyone knows it is working, and who is accountable. That is a
design problem, not an engineering problem.

Deliverables: current-state and future-state workflow maps distinguishing where
human judgment is genuinely required from where it is merely habitual; role
redesign for every position the workflow touches; agentic workflow blueprints
with agent placement, human-in-the-loop checkpoints and escalation paths; an
evaluation framework for every AI-assisted decision point; a RACI matrix and
traceability diagrams for governance and audit; and a pilot action plan that
bridges to implementation.

## Tier three, Forward-Deployed Design Engineering

**Monthly · minimum 3 months**

Skowak works inside the client's team. The system gets designed, built,
instrumented and proved, and Skowak stays until it is in production, the
client's people can maintain it, and there is a plan for what comes next. This
is not advisory, Skowak writes code, reviews the team's code, and owns delivery.

Deliverables: a production AI system built end to end; evaluation harnesses;
a performance dashboard tracking accuracy, reliability, adoption and business
impact; interface and workflow design; monthly retrospectives; team upskilling
throughout as the client's engineers build alongside Skowak; and a scale-up
roadmap with ownership transfer in the final month.

Month-to-month after the initial three months. If the evaluation dashboard shows
the work is not producing results, the client stops.

## AI Fluency

Three programs, not a fourth engagement tier: not building the system, but
making sure the people around it can evaluate it, govern it, and work
alongside it — or build a credentialed pipeline of people who can.

**Executive AI Fluency** (half-day intensive, leadership teams and boards,
cohort up to 12): decision competency, not hands-on tool use. Capability
assessment, use-case evaluation, risk and governance literacy, human-AI
orchestration, and a
rehearsed answer to the board-level AI-strategy question.

**Builder AI Fluency** (multi-week embedded series, engineering and delivery
teams): the same Evaluation-Driven Design practices Skowak uses on its own
engagements, taught to a client's engineers so an embedded engagement's
ownership transfer actually holds. Runs best alongside an active
Forward-Deployed Design Engineering engagement, but does not require one.

**AI Apprenticeship & Workforce Development** (program design engagement,
enterprise, government and workforce partners): Skowak designs the AI
instructional and competency components of a structured apprenticeship or
workforce-development program, Related Technical Instruction (RTI),
competency frameworks, curriculum, mentor and supervisor training, and
assessment, rather than running the program itself. For organizations scaling
AI capability across many roles through an existing or planned apprenticeship
structure, not a single team.

Program models range from an AI Workforce Assessment that establishes priorities,
to full AI Apprenticeship and RTI Development, to Mentor and Supervisor
Certification for organizations whose curriculum is already in place.

## What every engagement produces

Working software in the client's repository under their control; evaluation
suites and ground-truth datasets they own; architecture and decision
documentation; interface designs and workflow models; recorded walkthroughs for
the team who inherits it; and a written record of what was ruled out and why.
